  A psychometric audit of how the field measures robot foundation models —
  Bayesian item-response theory over 19 models and 556 tasks pooled from eight
  papers. About 29% of tasks carry near-zero discriminative information, and
  IRT and Bradley–Terry rankings agree at only ρ ≈ 0.17. CS 321M.
