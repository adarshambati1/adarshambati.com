I work on the gap between robots that run in a lab and robots anyone can afford
to deploy. That splits into three things I keep coming back to: building
manipulation systems out of deliberately cheap hardware, training policies for
contact-rich tasks, and questioning whether the benchmarks we grade those
policies on measure anything at all.

Before this I co-founded two companies with the same two people — Sophris, which
checked circuit-board schematics with AI, and Candor Security, which we took
through Y Combinator.
